# Resident Daily Planning Policy

This policy is the standing reference for AI-controlled residents in timed villages.

## Rules

1. **Plan after waking.** Review at least today and tomorrow's meals, bed/work limits, existing commitments, personally known orders, local stock and the bounded demand forecast before productive work.
2. **Use real evidence.** Forecasts and recipes create requirements, not stock. Only physical inventory, accepted orders, observed work and completed deliveries are facts of execution.
3. **Assume provisionally, confirm physically.** Initial plans may assume an upstream supplier can cover required goods. Contact that supplier through an allowed conversation, board or inbox to confirm terms, deadline and quantity.
4. **Replan on material change.** New orders, acceptance, rejection, deferral, shortage, late delivery, broken equipment or missed production trigger a new plan. Unchanged observations do not create duplicate plans.
5. **Notify downstream customers.** If a supplier discovers it cannot meet an accepted or proposed customer order, it must communicate the adjustment requirement rather than silently retain the original promise.
6. **Protect capacity and time.** Include travel, batch duration, meal/service windows, rest, fuel, tools, livestock care and transport capacity before promising work.
7. **Expose an auditable rationale.** Timeline entries may show inputs, calculations, assumptions, chosen actions and blockers. They must not claim access to hidden internal chain-of-thought, remote stock or another resident's private plan.
8. **Preserve privacy.** Residents retain only personally known orders, conversations and direct observations. Operator dashboards may aggregate projections, but NPC planning must not use the dashboard as oracle knowledge.
9. **Expose the bill of materials.** Each planned output names its customer/service, order IDs, quantity, deadline, components, on-hand stock, confirmed incoming quantity, shortfall, supplier and confirmation status.
